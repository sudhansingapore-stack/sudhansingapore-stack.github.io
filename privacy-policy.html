Privacy Policy for tictock

Last updated: 20 November 2026

tictock is a personal application built by Sudhakumarsukumaan to upload videos to the developer's own TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account information: when you connect your TikTok account, TikTok shares your basic profile information (such as your TikTok username and profile picture) and an access token that lets the app post on your behalf.
  • Video files: videos you choose to upload are sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API so they can be published or saved as drafts on your account.

How data is used

Your account information and access token are used only to authenticate with TikTok and to upload the videos you choose. The app does not collect data from anyone else, does not track visitors, and does not show advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third party, except TikTok itself, which is necessary to perform the upload you requested. No data is shared for advertising or analytics.

Data retention

Access tokens are stored only for as long as needed to keep your TikTok account connected. You can disconnect the app at any time from your TikTok settings, which revokes the app's access. Video files are sent to TikTok and are not kept by the app after the upload finishes.

Children

This app is for the developer's own use and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
